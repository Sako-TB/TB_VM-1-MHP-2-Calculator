    SECTION 2: Hazards identification

    2.1 Classification (Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008, CLP)

    Acute toxicity, oral, Cat. 4H302 — Harmful if swallowed.
    STOT RE, Cat. 2 (kidneys, oral)H373 — May cause damage to organs (kidneys) through prolonged or repeated exposure.

    The mixture is not classified for flammability (flash point > 93 °C; see Sections 5 and 9). Classification is driven by the ethylene glycol content (harmonised classification, Annex VI to CLP, Index No 603-027-00-1).

    2.2 Label elements

    Signal word: Warning — Pictograms: GHS07 (exclamation mark), GHS08 (health hazard). Official pictogram artwork per CLP Annex V must be used on labels.

    Hazard statements: H302 Harmful if swallowed. H373 May cause damage to organs (kidneys) through prolonged or repeated exposure.

    Precautionary statements: P260 Do not breathe mist/vapours/spray. P264 Wash hands thoroughly after handling. P270 Do not eat, drink or smoke when using this product. P280 Wear protective gloves/protective clothing/eye protection/face protection. P301+P312 IF SWALLOWED: Call a POISON CENTER/doctor if you feel unwell. P314 Get medical advice/attention if you feel unwell. P330 Rinse mouth. P403+P235 Store in a well-ventilated place. Keep cool. P501 Dispose of contents/container to an approved hazardous-waste collection point in accordance with local, regional and national regulations.

    2.3 Other hazards

    The mixture does not contain PBT or vPvB substances. No endocrine-disrupting properties are known for the components. Dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) readily penetrates intact skin and can carry dissolved substances through the skin; avoid all skin contact, in particular with used/contaminated solution. Spilled product is slippery.

    SECTION 4: First-aid measures

    General adviceConsult a physician and show this safety data sheet. Move affected person out of the danger area.
    If inhaledMove to fresh air. Call a poison centre or doctor if you feel unwell.
    Skin contactRemove contaminated clothing. Wash off immediately with soap and plenty of water. Get medical attention if symptoms occur. Note: DMSO promotes absorption of substances through the skin.
    Eye contactRinse thoroughly with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Continue rinsing during transport and consult a physician.
    If swallowedRinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting unless directed by medical personnel. If vomiting occurs naturally, lean the person forward to reduce aspiration risk. Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. Call a poison centre or doctor.
    4.2 Symptoms/effectsIngestion of significant quantities may cause nausea, CNS depression, metabolic acidosis and acute kidney injury due to the ethylene glycol content; onset of renal effects may be delayed by 12–24 hours. Skin absorption of DMSO can cause a transient garlic-like taste/breath odour and mild skin irritation.
    4.3 Medical attentionTreat symptomatically. For significant ethylene glycol ingestion, consider antidotal therapy (fomepizole; alternatively ethanol) and monitor acid–base status and renal function.

    SECTION 5: Fire-fighting measures

    5.1 Extinguishing mediaWater spray, alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical or carbon dioxide. No extinguishing media known to be unsuitable.
    5.2 Special hazardsThe product is not classified as flammable (flash point > 93 °C, closed cup; high water content). In the event of fire, thermal decomposition may release carbon oxides (CO, CO₂) and sulphur oxides (SOₓ, from DMSO).
    5.3 Advice for firefightersWear self-contained breathing apparatus and protective clothing. Use water spray to cool unopened containers exposed to fire.

    SECTION 6: Accidental release measures

    6.1 Personal precautionsUse personal protective equipment as described in Section 8. Avoid contact with skin, eyes and clothing. Ensure adequate ventilation. Spilled product is slippery. Evacuate non-essential personnel.
    6.2 Environmental precautionsPrevent further leakage or spillage if safe to do so. Do not allow product to enter drains, surface water or groundwater.
    6.3 Containment / cleaning upSoak up with inert absorbent material (e.g. sand, diatomaceous earth, universal binder). Collect in suitable, closed and labelled containers and dispose of as hazardous waste (see Section 13).
    6.4 Reference to other sectionsSee Section 8 for personal protection and Section 13 for disposal.

    SECTION 7: Handling and storage

    7.1 Safe handlingAvoid contact with skin and eyes — DMSO carries dissolved substances through intact skin (see 2.3). Use the personal protective equipment specified in Section 8. Keep containers closed when not in use. Do not eat, drink or smoke while handling. Wash hands after handling. Never return spills to original containers for re-use.
    7.2 Safe storageKeep containers tightly closed and upright in a cool, dry, well-ventilated place. Store away from strong oxidising agents. Suitable container materials: HDPE, glass, stainless steel. Keep out of the reach of unauthorised persons.
    7.3 Specific end use(s)See identified uses in Section 1.2. No further specific uses stipulated.

    8.2 Exposure controls

    Engineering controlsUse ventilation adequate to keep airborne concentrations below the exposure limits. Eye-wash facility recommended in the work area.
    Eye/face protectionTightly fitting safety goggles (EN 166).
    Hand protectionChemical-resistant gloves per EN ISO 374-1. Recommended material: butyl rubber (breakthrough time > 480 min). Note: nitrile provides only short-term splash protection against DMSO-containing liquids — replace gloves immediately after contact.
    Body protectionProtective clothing (lab coat/apron) selected according to the activity and amount handled.
    Respiratory protectionNot required under normal use with adequate ventilation. Where mists or vapours exceed exposure limits, use a respirator with type ABEK-P cartridges (EN 14387).
    Environmental controlsDo not let product enter drains or watercourses.

    MixtureClassified Acute Tox. 4 (oral), H302, based on the ethylene glycol content and the CLP calculation method (converted acute toxicity estimate).
    Skin / eyeSkin corrosion/irritation: not classified; may cause mild, transient irritation. Serious eye damage/irritation: not classified based on available component data.
    Sensitisation / mutagenicityNot classified. Germ cell mutagenicity: not classified.
    CarcinogenicityNo component present at ≥ 0.1 % is identified as a carcinogen by IARC, NTP or OSHA.
    Reproductive toxicityNot classified under CLP. Note: ethylene glycol is listed under California Proposition 65 for developmental toxicity (ingestion route).
    STOTSingle exposure: not classified. Repeated exposure: STOT RE 2 (H373) — may cause damage to the kidneys through prolonged or repeated oral exposure (ethylene glycol ≥ 10 %).
    Aspiration hazardNot classified.
    OtherDMSO enhances percutaneous absorption of co-formulants and contaminants; treat skin contact with used solution accordingly.
